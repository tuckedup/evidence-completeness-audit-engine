# Manual adjudication protocol

Sample 150–300 records after automated evaluation. Stratify jointly by check family and
flag/no-flag outcome, then randomize display order. Two reviewers independently inspect the
source report and structured record while blinded to one another's decision. Resolve
disagreements by consensus or a third reviewer. Record exclusions and missing-source cases;
never silently relabel them.

The automated agreement report is not a substitute for this review. Report agreement against
the deterministic structured-field labeler and against adjudicated labels separately, with the
sampling frame, confusion matrices, Cohen's kappa, Gwet's AC1, and bootstrap confidence
intervals.
